Zheng-Jun Du (杜正君)

Zheng-Jun Du is currently an associate professor with the School of Computer Science and Technology at Qinghai University, and he directs the institute of Computational Visual Media. He got a PhD degree in Computer Science from Tsinghua University, supervised by Professor Kun Xu. His research interests mainly include image & video processing, computer graphics, computer vision, and astronomical data processing.
